How the areas compare

Every location is measured with the same 5-mile circle. “Only in this circle” counts the high-income households that the other two picks do not also reach.

How the three were chosen

Every point on a quarter-mile grid inside Mesa city limits was tested as a circle center, looking for the three circles that together cover the most households earning $150,000 or more. Centers were kept inside Mesa because the office needs a Mesa address; the circles themselves reach into Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert and Queen Creek. The best possible set reaches about 87,400. Moving each center to the nearest major intersection (Dobson and Guadalupe, Power and Pecos, Higley and University) costs about 1,100 of them.

West Mesa (Dobson Ranch)

The circle around Dobson Road and Guadalupe Road covers Dobson Ranch, west and central Mesa south of the Salt River, central and east Tempe, north Chandler and the northwest corner of Gilbert. It holds about 34,000 households earning $150,000 or more, the most of the three, and 36% of its homes were built before 1980, which means more repair and repipe work. The trade-off is competition: 114 plumbers list a street address within 5 miles, about 300 high-income households each, including Benjamin Franklin Plumbing on McQueen Road in Gilbert and Mr. Rooter on Country Club Drive in Mesa. Only 20% of households earn $150,000 or more and 51% own their home, so much of the circle is rental housing.

Southeast Mesa (Gateway)

The Gateway circle, centered on Power Road and Pecos Road just west of Phoenix-Mesa Gateway Airport, takes in Eastmark, the Gateway area, southeast Gilbert and north Queen Creek. 41% of its households earn $150,000 or more and 74% own their home, the best mix of the three. Only 18 plumbers list a street address within 5 miles, about 1,580 high-income households each, and none is a national franchise. Its homes are new: 3% were built before 1980.

East Mesa

The East Mesa circle, centered on Higley Road and University Drive, runs from Stapley Drive to Ellsworth Road and south to about Elliot Road. It includes Falcon Field, the Power Road corridor and Superstition Springs. 72% of households own their home and 27% of homes were built before 1980. It has 71 plumbers with a street address, about 350 high-income households each. It shares about 1,100 high-income households with the Gateway circle and almost none with West Mesa.

Pros and cons at a glance

West Mesa (Dobson Ranch)

Pros

  • Largest market: 34,000 high-income households and 169,700 households in total.
  • Oldest housing: 36% built before 1980.
  • Almost no overlap with the other two circles.

Cons

  • Most crowded: 114 plumbers, about 300 high-income households each.
  • Two national franchises inside the circle: Benjamin Franklin Plumbing and Mr. Rooter.
  • Lowest share earning $150k+ (20%) and 51% owner-occupied.

Southeast Mesa (Gateway)

Pros

  • Best ratio by far: 18 plumbers, about 1,580 high-income households each.
  • Richest mix: 41% earn $150k+ and 74% own their home.
  • No national franchise inside the circle.

Cons

  • Smallest total market: 69,300 households.
  • Newest homes (3% before 1980), so less old-pipe repair work.
  • Few small offices for rent nearby (see the Offices page).

East Mesa

Pros

  • 24,900 high-income households and 116,600 households in total.
  • 72% owner-occupied; 27% of homes built before 1980.
  • About 23,800 of its high-income households are not in either other circle.

Cons

  • 71 plumbers with a street address, about 350 high-income households each.
  • Only 21% of households earn $150k+.
  • Shares about 1,100 high-income households with Gateway.

What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on a lobby or building directory from the first month? No listing publishes this.
  2. Can the company name go on the office door itself, not just a monument sign? This is also never published.
  3. Is the office available now, and will you do month-to-month with no minimum? Ask for the month-to-month price in writing.
  4. Is the office on the ground floor, with its own door from the parking lot or a short walk from the front entrance?
  5. Is the front desk staffed during business hours, will mail addressed to the suite be accepted, and can someone verify the address by video or in person?

How this was put together

Business confirmed. The phone number on eastvalleyplumbingrepair.com, (480) 255-7325, matches the contact for East Valley Plumbing Repair in monday.com. The website lists 2205 S. Cactus Rd., Apache Junction as its address; following the method, that address was not treated as already covering Mesa. The account notes in monday.com ask to focus on Dreamland Villa, Leisure World, Fountain of the Sun and Sun City. The first three are inside the East Mesa circle, within about 4 miles of its center.

Circle centers. West Mesa: Dobson Rd and Guadalupe Rd. Southeast Mesa: Power Rd and Pecos Rd. East Mesa: Higley Rd and University Dr. Intersections were placed from Census TIGER road lines.

Choosing the areas. Every point on a quarter-mile grid inside Mesa city limits was scored on the number of $150,000+ households within 5 miles, and the three centers that together cover the most of them (each household counted once) were found by search. The best set reaches about 87,400; the three named centers reach about 86,300. Circles centered outside Mesa can do better (see the alternates on page 2), but were not considered.

Households. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 5-year estimates by census tract for Maricopa and Pinal counties: household income (table B19001, the $150,000 to $199,999 and $200,000+ brackets), owner-occupied homes (B25003) and year the structure was built (B25034). Each tract’s households are spread evenly over its land area, with lakes, rivers and canals removed, and the share of each tract inside a circle is counted. Tract estimates carry sampling error, so treat figures as plus or minus a few percent.

Competitors. Better Business Bureau listings in the Plumber category, sorted by distance, pulled for 12 search points across Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe and Queen Creek (the first 225 results of each, 546 unique listings). A business counts if its name or main category is plumbing, drain, sewer, rooter or water heater work and it lists a street address. PO boxes, private mailboxes (for example “Ste 105-445” at a mail store) and listings with no address were left out. Each address was placed with the Census geocoder, falling back to BBB’s own map point where the geocoder found no match. Google Maps listings were not used, and plumbers with no BBB listing are missing from every circle.

Offices. Small private offices within about 6 miles of each center, found on listing sites (Cityfeet, Showcase, LoopNet, Crexi, OfficeSpace.com, CoworkingCafe, CommercialSearch, CommercialCafe, Office Hub, LiquidSpace) and operator pages, checked November 3, 2026. Some sites could not be opened and were read from search results instead. None of the offices were visited or called. Ratings are green only when a listing or the operator says so.

Map. Census tracts, freeways, highways and water from Census TIGERweb. Shading is households earning $150,000 or more per square mile of land. The color scale is higher than in reports for smaller metros because East Valley suburbs are denser.
